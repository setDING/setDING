The band leader puts the set together once, and everyone in the band has it on their tablet, phone or computer – the right songs, in the right order, in the right key. On every stage, even without Wi-Fi.

MADE FOR THE STAGE
• Live mode in full screen; the screen stays on for the whole gig
• Turn pages with a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth foot pedal
• Your keyboard switches to the right sound for every song over MIDI
• Works completely offline

SETS THE WHOLE BAND SHARES
• Drag and drop songs into a set, add breaks and notes
• Set the key and the sequence per song
• Every member gets the set on their device automatically
• setDING shows when something is still missing for a gig without a network
• Choose who plays a set, substitutes from other bands included

MADE FOR DRUMMERS TOO
Tempo, time signature and song sequence instead of chords, a count-in and a tight click on your in-ears.

EVERY MUSICIAN SEES IT THEIR WAY
• Transpose with one tap, set a capo, change the font size
• B or H notation, Nashville numbers and Roman numerals
• Cues just for your instrument or your voice
• Freehand notes right on the sheet
None of it changes the song for the rest of the band.

YOUR SONGS, YOUR FORMAT
• Songs in the open ChordPro format
• Import them one by one or as a ZIP, attach PDF sheet music
• Melodies in ABC or LilyPond, language versions of a song
• Built-in editor with a live preview
• Report mistakes right on the song, with a marked-up screenshot

FOR BANDS, SCHOOLS AND CHURCHES
Several bands of an organization share one song library. Invite members with a link or a QR code. Roles decide who looks after the songs and who plans sets.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Without an account, everything stays on your device
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Servers in Switzerland – or run the server yourself, with Docker or without
• Free and open source under the MIT licence

Guide and more: https://setding.app/en/
