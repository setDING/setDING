Songs are written in [ChordPro](https://www.chordpro.org/), the open format for
chord sheets: chords go in square brackets right inside the lyrics, details such as
the title or key in curly brackets. [ChordPro syntax](/en/songs/chordpro/) explains
the notation and the setDING directives for the song sequence and drums.

```
{title: Amazing Grace}
{key: G}

{start_of_verse}
A[G]mazing [G7]grace, how [C]sweet the [G]sound
{end_of_verse}
```

Open the editor with **New song**, or with **Edit ChordPro** in a song's ⋮ menu.

<Image src={editor} alt="The ChordPro editor with insert menus, syntax highlighting and a live preview" />

## Text and preview

On wide screens the text and the preview are side by side; on narrow ones you switch
between **Text** and **Preview**. The preview shows the song the way the band sees
it.

## Insert instead of typing

The bar above the text inserts what you need without you having to know the
ChordPro syntax:

- **Insert chord**, **Insert verse**, **Insert chorus** and **Insert comment** right
  in the bar.
- **Chords** – e.g. **Optional chord**, **No chord (N.C.)**, **Chord diagram**.
- **Sections** – intro, bridge, solo, outro and more, **Tab**, **Chord grid** and
  **Repeat chorus**.
- **Song info** – **Title**, **Artist**, **Key**, **Capo**, **Tempo (BPM)**, **Time
  signature**, **Song sequence**, **CCLI number** and more.
- **Text** – comments, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Colour** and more.
- **More** – **Column break**, **Page break**, **Image**, **ABC notation** and more.

**Song details** edits the title, key, tempo and other details in a form. While you
type, the editor suggests matching chords and directives.

## Edit lines

**Lines** has **Move line up**, **Move line down**, **Duplicate line**, **Delete
line** and **Comment out line (#)**, plus **Move chord left** and **Move chord
right** to move a chord by one syllable. Tapping a line number selects the whole
line. **Undo** and **Redo** step back and forth.

## Search and replace

**Search** finds text in the song, with **Match case** if you like. **Replace**
replaces single matches, **Replace all** all of them.

## Chords above lyrics

Many chord sheets on the internet write the chords on a line of their own above the
lyrics. When you paste such a text, setDING offers to convert it (**Convert**). For
text that is already in the editor: **Convert chords above lyrics**.

## Save

**Save** saves the song for everyone. If setDING finds places that will probably not
be shown as intended – e.g. a bracket that is never closed – it asks **Check the
ChordPro?** and names the lines. You can **Fix automatically**, **Keep editing** or
**Save anyway**.

To see everything ChordPro can do, import the sample song
[`feature-showcase.zip`](https://github.com/tafli/setDING/raw/main/docs/examples/feature-showcase.zip)
and open it in the editor.
