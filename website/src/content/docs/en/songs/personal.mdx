When you open a song, there are three icons at the top:

- **Personal adjustments** – how *you* see the song.
- **Attachments** – PDF sheet music for the song, with their number.
- **Band arrangement** – how the selected band plays the song
  (see [Key and sequence](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/)).

## Personal adjustments

**Just for you – the song stays unchanged for everyone else.** Your adjustments
belong to your account and apply on all your devices, in every set:

- **My key – just for me**: transposes the song, e.g. for your vocal range.
- **Capo**: sets a capo; the chords are rewritten to match. **No capo** plays without
  one, even if the song asks for one.
- **Smaller text** / **Larger text** for this song.
- **Nashville numbers**: shows this song with scale degrees instead of chord names.
- **Chords in verses**: how verses that share their chords show them. A verse is a
  `{start_of_verse}` section or one with a name such as "Verse 2".
  - **Chords in all verses**: as written.
  - **Chords in verse 1 only**: the other verses show the lyrics alone. This saves
    room on small screens.
  - **Verses stacked**: like in a hymnal, each line of chords appears once, and
    below it the same line of every verse, numbered 1., 2., …, so the words under a
    chord line up. The stacked verses appear where the first one was; a chorus in
    between stays where it is. Verses without chords of their own join with their
    text in one piece below. Verses with other chords, or with comments, tabs or
    images, stay on their own.
- **My notes**: a note only you see, e.g. "come in after 4 bars". It appears with
  the song, in live mode too.
- **Freehand annotations**: how many drawings you have on the song, a switch to hide
  them for this song, and **Delete all annotations of this song**. You draw them in
  live mode, see [Freehand annotations](/en/live/annotations/).

A dot on the icon shows that you see the song differently from how it is written.
**Reset personal adjustments** undoes everything.

When you play a song in a set, your adjustment comes on top of the set's key: if the
set transposes a whole tone up and you a semitone down, you see the song a semitone
higher than the band.

## PDF attachments

You can attach PDF sheet music to any song, e.g. the score for the horns. It is
synced with the song and available offline on every device.

1. Open the song and tap **Attachments**.
2. Tap **Attach file** and choose a PDF or an image. A file may be at most 20 MB.
3. In the attachment's menu you can **Rename** or **Delete** it.

Everybody who may edit the song may attach and delete files.

### PDF in live mode

Under **Show in live mode** you choose whether you see the **ChordPro text** or one
of the PDFs in live mode. The choice is yours alone. So the guitarist plays from the
chord sheet and the trumpeter from their part – in the same set. A PDF is turned
with the same gestures as a chord sheet.

To look at a PDF or image without changing this choice, tap the eye icon next to
it. A PDF opens in live mode, an image full screen.

How much space the PDFs take on the device is shown in **Settings** → **Storage &
info**.
