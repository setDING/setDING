An imported song looks finished, but often is not: a wrong chord, a missing
verse, a different key than the one your band plays. setDING therefore keeps
track of the songs **to review**, and everyone in the organization can
**report a mistake** when they notice one in rehearsal or on stage.

Who may do what:

- **Report a mistake**: everyone in the organization, members too.
- **Approve** songs, **mark them for review** and **resolve** reports: whoever
  may edit the song, i.e. the **librarians** and admins of the organization,
  and **band leaders** for their own songs.
- **See** reports: members see their own, leaders, set planners, librarians
  and admins all of them.

## Checking after an import

Every song you import from a file (also from SongSelect) is **To review** at
first, with the reason **After import**. Archives (backups, set and song
archives) bring the status the songs had in the archive; songs written by hand
are not to review.

In the song list a small icon shows everyone that a song is not checked yet or
has a reported mistake. Whoever may edit songs finds the lines **songs to
review** and **songs with reported mistakes** under **To do** on the overview;
they open the filtered song list. The list **To review** offers **Approve
all**, with **Undo**.

<Image src={list} alt="The song list filtered to the songs to review, with Approve all" />

In the song, a banner shows why it is to review and how many reports are open.
**Approve** approves it; with open reports, setDING asks whether to resolve
them too. Saving alone never approves a song. In the menu (⋮), **Mark for
review …** with a reason (e.g. "the bridge is wrong") puts a song back on the
list.

<Image src={banner} alt="A song with the banner To review: After import and one open report" />

Everyone else sees a short hint in the song that it is not checked yet or that
a mistake was reported. You can still play it: nothing blocks a song in sets or
live mode. A set with an upcoming gig shows how many of its songs are to review
or have a reported mistake.

## Reporting a mistake

**Report a mistake …** in the menu (⋮) of a song, or the icon in the control
bar of live mode, opens the form: choose what it is about (**Chords**,
**Lyrics**, **Key**, **Sequence**, **Tempo**, **Formatting**, **Sheet music
(PDF)**, **Other**), add a **Comment** if you like and tap **Send**. In live
mode the page you are on is added. On stage one press of the pedal is enough:
with a category chosen it sends the report, without one it cancels.

<Image src={sheet} alt="The Report a mistake form with the categories and a comment" />

The report is stored on your device and sent with the next sync, also when you
are offline.

If the server does not accept a new report (for example because you no longer
have the permission), it stays on your device and setDING tells you once. In the
song's **Mistake reports** it is marked **Only on this device** with the reason.
It is not sent again with every sync: **Send again** sends it with the next sync,
for example once you have the permission again; **Withdraw** removes it from your
device.

<Image src={refused} alt="A mistake report with “Only on this device: no permission to report mistakes” and Send again" />

### With a screenshot

**Attach a screenshot** shows exactly what you saw: setDING takes the current
view, and you mark the place with the **Pen** or the **Highlighter**.
**Attach** adds the image to the report. Since the screenshot shows your view
(font size, transposition, capo), the librarians also see whether the mistake
is perhaps in your personal adjustments.

<Image src={marking} alt="A screenshot with a red circle around the chord" />

## Resolving reports

The icon with the number in the title bar of a song (or **Show** in the banner)
opens the **Mistake reports**: open ones first, resolved and withdrawn ones
below as history. A tap on a screenshot shows it full size.

- Whoever reported can **withdraw** an open report.
- Whoever may edit the song **resolves** it with an **Answer** (e.g. "Fixed,
  thanks!") or **reopens** it.

After the next sync, the reporter is told once that the report was resolved,
with the answer.
