All songs belong to the organization; all its bands share the same song library.
**Songs** shows them.

Who may do what:

- **Create and import** songs: the organization's **Librarian** and admins, and the
  **Leader** of a band.
- **Edit** songs: librarians and admins. A leader may edit a song they created or
  imported themselves, as long as nobody else has changed it since.
- **Delete** songs: librarians and admins.

Everybody else sees and plays the songs and adjusts them
[just for themselves](/en/songs/personal/).

## Find songs

- **Search songs** finds songs by title and artist, and also by their lyrics
  (**Found in the lyrics**).
- **Sort** by **Title**, **Artist**, **Key** or **Recently edited**.
- If songs have tags (e.g. "Christmas"), **Tags** above the list filters by them.
  You can tick several tags; the list then shows the songs with any of the chosen
  tags. **All** shows all songs again.

Small icons next to a song show whether it has PDF attachments, whether it is sheet
music (PDF) only, in which upcoming set it is played and whether the selected band
has an arrangement for it.

If you are in several organizations, switch at the top with **Switch song library**.

## Languages

setDING recognises the language of a song from its lyrics: German, English, French,
Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Dutch. When the library has songs in more than one
language, the list shows a small code after the title (**DE**, **EN**, **CH**, …)
and offers a language filter next to the order; **All languages** shows all songs
again.

<Image src={songsLanguages} alt="The song list with language codes after the titles (FR, CH, DE, EN) and the All languages filter" />

If the lyrics are short, mix two languages evenly or are written in a dialect, no
language may be recognised, or the wrong one. Whoever may edit the song sets it
by hand under **⋮ → Language …**, also **Swiss German** or **Other**.
**Automatic** goes back to the recognised language. A language set by hand
always wins and reaches the other devices with the next sync.

<Image src={songLanguage} alt="The Language dialog of a song: Automatic: German, and the list of languages with Swiss German chosen" />

## Language versions

Many songs exist in several languages: a translation, or a free adaptation in
Swiss German. Link them, and every version shows the others below its header;
a tap opens that version. The language of the app comes first.

<Image src={languageVersions} alt="A song with the chips English (selected), German and Swiss German below its header" />

Whoever may edit the song links it under **⋮ → Link language version …** and picks
the other song; songs with the same artist, key, tempo or sequence are listed
first. Linking a song that already has versions adds it to them.
**⋮ → Remove from the language versions** takes a song out again, with **Undo**.
In the song list, a translate icon marks
songs with other language versions.

<Image src={languageVersionPicker} alt="Choose a language version: the songs that can be linked, with their language codes" />

Every version stays a song of its own, with its own key, arrangements and
personal adjustments. A song in the trash is not shown as a version; restored, it
is linked again.

## Write a song

- **New song** opens the [ChordPro editor](/en/songs/editor/).
- **New PDF song** creates a song with just a title, to which you then
  [attach](/en/songs/personal/#pdf-attachments) sheet music – for pieces that only
  exist as sheet music.

## Import ChordPro files

1. On **Songs**, tap **Import files**.
2. Supported are `.cho`, `.chordpro`, `.chopro`, `.crd`, `.pro`, `.txt` and ZIP
   archives containing such files – including the ChordPro files you download from
   SongSelect. Their CCLI number becomes `{x_ccli}`; the licence number of your
   church (`{ccli_license}`) is not copied into the song. The CCLI footer after
   the song (song number, copyright, terms of use, licence number) is removed;
   song number and copyright go into the song details.
3. If the files write H instead of B, select **Files use German notation (H/B)**.
   The chords are then converted on import.
4. Tap **Choose files**.

setDING checks the imported songs. If they contain places that will probably not be
shown as intended, e.g. a bracket that is not closed, **Check the import** appears.
**Fix automatically** repairs the places with a clear fix; fix the rest in the
editor. These songs are marked with a warning icon in the list.

Songs that write section labels in the short form `{start_of_verse: Verse 1}` are
listed there too: **Convert labels** writes them as `label="…"`, as the ChordPro
specification recommends. They look the same either way.

<Image src={importLabels} alt="Check the import: one song with a place to fix, two songs with short-form section labels, and the buttons Convert labels and Fix automatically" />

## Convert songs

Some songs show as intended but do not follow the ChordPro specification yet, or
still contain parts of a SongSelect file: `{ccli}` and `{ccli_license}`, the CCLI
footer, CCLI bar lines that can become a grid, or section labels in the short form.
The editor offers to convert each of these. So that you find these songs, the song
list marks them with a magic wand icon, and the **To do** card on the **Overview**
counts them (**songs with possible conversions**). Only people who may edit the song
see them.

A tap on the magic wand converts that song at once; **Undo** in the message brings
it back. To convert several songs, choose the filter **Convertible** above the song
list, or tap the line on the overview. **Convert all** then converts all of them at
once, for everyone who uses the songs.

The other lines of **To do** are filters of the song list as well: **ChordPro
problems**, **Sync conflicts** and **Sheet music only** appear above the list when
there are such songs; tap a filter again to show all songs.

<Image src={songsConversions} alt="The song list filtered to Convertible: two songs with the magic wand button and the Convert all button" />

## Export, copy, delete

In a song's ⋮ menu:

- **Export ChordPro file** saves only the song's text as a ChordPro file.
- **Export with attachments** saves the song with its attachments as a ZIP that
  setDING imports again (see below).
- **Copy to band** copies the song into the song library of another organization in
  which you may create songs. Bands of the same organization share their songs
  anyway. Several songs at once: the song list's ⋮ menu → **Select**.
- **Delete** deletes the song and removes it from all sets. Right afterwards,
  **Undo** brings it back.

Several songs at once: the song list's ⋮ menu → **Select**, then tap the songs
(or long-press a song). At the top are **Select all**, **Copy to band**,
**Export with attachments** and, if you may delete songs, **Delete selected**. After you confirm, the songs go to the
trash; **Undo** brings all of them back.

<Image src={songsSelected} alt="The song list with two selected songs and, at the top, the buttons Select all, Copy to band and Delete selected" />

## Pass on songs with their attachments

**Export with attachments** – in a song's ⋮ menu, or for selected songs in the song
list – saves the songs as a ZIP archive: each song as a ChordPro file, plus the
attachments and a description from which setDING imports the songs again. Before
saving, you choose what goes into the archive:

- **Attachments** – PDFs and images of the songs.
- **Arrangements** of the current band – key, sequence and tempo.
- **My personal adjustments and notes** – off by default. Leave this off if you pass
  the file on.

The language of each song and which songs are
[language versions](#language-versions) of each other always come along: songs
that were linked are linked again when they are imported as new songs. Only the
songs you selected are exported, not their other versions.

<Image src={songExport} alt="The Export song dialog with the options for attachments, arrangements and personal adjustments" />

To import, choose the ZIP under **Import files**. Before anything is saved, setDING
shows a preview: which songs are already here, which are new and which are here with
a different text – then you choose whether to keep the existing ones or import the
songs as well. If a song that is already here lacks an attachment from the archive,
it is **added**, provided you may edit the song; existing attachments with the same
name stay as they are. This also applies when you import a set.

<Image src={songImport} alt="The Import songs preview: one song already here, one new song and one attachment added to an existing song" />

## Trash

Deleted songs can be restored for **30 days**: the song list's ⋮ menu → **Trash**
→ **Restore**. The song comes back with the attachments deleted with it, its
personal adjustments and band arrangements. It is no longer in sets; add it there
again. Whoever may delete songs sees the trash. After 30 days a deleted song is
removed for good.

## Conflicts

If two people changed the same song at the same time, one of them offline, the song
says **This song was changed on the server and on this device.** Compare with
**Show server version**, then decide: **Keep mine**, **Use server version** or
**Keep both** (your version becomes a song of its own).

## Back up the library

The song list's ⋮ menu → **Back up library** saves the whole library as a ZIP file:
all songs as ChordPro files (readable without setDING too), the attachments, the
sets with their items, the band arrangements and your own personal adjustments and
notes. Anybody can back up the library *On this device*; an organization's library
is backed up by its librarians and admins, in the browser too.

Before backing up an organization, setDING syncs with the server so that the backup
contains everything. Without a connection, with changes not uploaded yet, with
conflicts or with attachments not downloaded yet, it stops with a message.

To **restore**, import the ZIP file like other files (**Import files**). Everything
comes in as a copy into the current library; nothing existing is changed. Songs that
already exist with the same text are not added twice. If a song exists with a
different text, you choose whether to keep the existing one or to import the backup
as an additional song; setDING lists the additional songs afterwards so that you can
compare and clean up. For each band of the backup you choose which band gets its
sets.
