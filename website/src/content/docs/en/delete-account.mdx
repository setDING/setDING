This page explains how to delete your account in **setDING**, the app for the
chords of a band's songs, published by the setDING project (see the
[legal notice](/en/legal-notice/)). It applies to accounts on the setDING server
`api.setding.app`, which the app uses by default.

## How to delete your account

You need your email address and password. Deleting needs a connection to the
server.

**In the app** (Android, Windows, Linux):

1. Open **Settings** → **Account & sync**.
2. At the very end, in the red **Danger zone**, tap **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and confirm with **Delete account**.

**Without the app:** sign in to the web client at
[app.setding.app](https://app.setding.app) with your email address and password
and take the same steps there. You do not need to install anything.

**By email:** if you can no longer sign in, write to **info@setDING.app** from the
email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We delete the account within
30 days and confirm it to you.

If you are the **only admin** of an organization, first make someone else admin or
close the organization (see [Organization](/en/bands/organizations/)); the app tells
you which organizations are affected.

## What is deleted

At once and for good:

- your account: name, email address and password,
- your personal adjustments, annotations and personal settings,
- your memberships in organizations and bands,
- the open invitations you created.

You are signed out on all devices. The device you delete the account on also
removes the data of your organizations; your local library on the device stays
until you remove the app.

## What is kept

- **Songs, attachments, sets and play history** you created or edited belong to
  your organization and stay with it for the other members. They no longer show
  who changed them last.
- **Backups** of the server keep a copy of the deleted data for up to **14 days**;
  then it is gone.
- **Server logs** (IP address, time, request) are deleted after 7 days.

More in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/). An account on a
[server you run yourself](/en/self-hosting/overview/) is deleted the same way; its
operator is responsible for its backups.
