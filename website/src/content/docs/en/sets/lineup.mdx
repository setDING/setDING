Not every gig is played by the whole band: someone is ill, someone is on holiday,
and a person from another band helps out. That is why every set has a
**lineup**: the people who play it.

## Who plays a set

A new set is played by the **whole band**. The lineup is shown in the set below
its date and venue: **Lineup · Whole band** with the names of the band members, or
the people chosen for this set. Substitutes are marked **(Substitute)**.

<Image src={setLineup} alt="A set with the line Lineup: Anna, Beat, Carla (Substitute)" />

If you are not planned, the set says **Not planned for you**. You still see the
set, for example to rehearse or to step in; but it does not count as your next
gig, and the song list does not mark its songs.

## Choosing the lineup

Whoever may edit sets (band leaders, set planners, organization admins) taps the
lineup line or chooses **Choose lineup** in the set's ⋮ menu:

- Under **Band members** every member is checked. Uncheck everyone who does not
  play this time.
- **Add people** searches the people of the organization. Whoever is not in the
  band is added as a **Substitute**. You also find the
  [people without an account](#people-without-an-account) there.
- **Whole band** resets the lineup. When every band member is checked again and
  there are no substitutes, the whole band plays it as well: the lineup follows
  the band again, new members included.

<Image src={lineupDialog} alt="The Lineup dialog with Anna and Beat checked, Dario unchecked and Carla as a substitute" />

The lineup is also printed in the header of the set list
([Print the set list](/en/sets/share/)).

## People without an account

Not everyone who plays uses setDING: the sound engineer, a guest musician,
someone who plays from the printed set list. So that the set still shows who took
part, add them to the lineup as **people without an account**. They belong to the
organization and can be chosen in every band.

<Image src={lineupContacts} alt="The Lineup dialog with the section People without an account and Marco Rossi, marked No account" />

- **Add people** shows the people of the organization and the people without an
  account together; these are marked **No account**. The search finds first name,
  last name and display name.
- **New person without an account** creates a person and puts them in the lineup
  right away, also without a connection. Only the first name is needed; the mail
  address is optional.

<Image src={lineupPickContact} alt="Add people with Elias of the organization, Lea Brunner without an account and the button New person without an account" />

<Image src={contactNew} alt="The dialog New person without an account with first name, last name, display name and mail address" />

In the set, on the set card and in the message to the lineup they are shown with
**(No account)**, on the printed set list with their name only. **Notify lineup**
writes to their mail address too.

### What setDING stores about them

For a person without an account setDING stores only the name and, if you enter
it, the mail address. Everyone in the organization sees the name; only people
who plan sets (band leaders, set planners, organization admins) see the mail
address. The person cannot open setDING and sees nothing.

### Managing and inviting them

Under **Settings** → **Organization & bands** → your organization, the section
**People without an account** lists all of them (if you plan sets). In a
person's ⋮ menu:

- **Edit** changes name and mail address (everyone who plans sets).
- **Invite to setDING** creates a **personal invitation** (organization admins;
  band leaders invite into their band). Show the link as a QR code, copy or share
  it, or send it **by mail** to the stored address. It works once, for 7 days.
- **Delete** (organization admins) takes the person out of the choice and
  removes their mail address at once. Sets that name them keep the name.

<Image src={contactInvite} alt="The personal invitation for Marco Rossi with QR code, link and the buttons Copy link, By mail and Share" />

When the person opens the link, the sign-up is filled in with their name and mail
address; they only add a password. With the new account they take over the sets
they were planned for, as a band member or [substitute](#as-a-substitute). If
they have an account already, they sign in and choose **Accept invitation**.

## Notifying the lineup

When the set is ready, **Notify lineup** in the set's ⋮ menu opens a prepared
message with the set, date, venue and lineup:

- **Share …** opens your device's share menu, for example for the band's chat.
- **Copy** puts the text on the clipboard.
- **By mail** opens your mail program with the lineup's addresses as recipients,
  those of the people without an account included. Without a connection to the
  server there are no recipients; then you choose them yourself.

setDING sends nothing itself: you decide how the message goes out.

<Image src={lineupNotify} alt="The Notify lineup dialog with the message and the buttons Copy, By mail and Share" />

## As a substitute

When you are in the lineup of a set of another band, that set comes to your
device with its songs and the band's key and sequence, offline as well. You see
only the sets you are planned for and cannot change them.

- The band appears in the band switcher with the role **Substitute**.
- On your own band's overview, the card **As a substitute** shows your upcoming
  gigs in other bands; a tap switches to the band and opens the set.
- When you are put in a lineup by name, the overview tells you once with
  **You are planned**; **Got it** hides the card.

<Image src={overviewSubstitute} alt="The overview with the cards You are planned and As a substitute for the set Summer party of the band Abendrot" />

When the band takes you out of the lineup, the set disappears from your device at
the next sync. After the gig it stays as long as you are in the lineup.
