A song is stored the way it was written. How your band plays it – in which key, in
which sequence, at which tempo – you set without changing the song. setDING has two
levels for this:

1. **Band arrangement:** how the band always plays the song. Applies in all the
   band's sets.
2. **Set:** what is different tonight, e.g. two semitones lower or without the
   second verse. Applies in this set only.

What is set in the set wins; otherwise the band arrangement applies, otherwise the
song. The band's **Leader** and **Set planner** and the admins of the organization
may change both. The other bands of the organization are not affected, and every
musician can still transpose or set a capo just for themselves on top.

## The band arrangement

1. On **Sets**, select the band.
2. Open the song on **Songs** and tap **Band arrangement** at the top.
3. Set:
   - the key with **Key of the band – for all its sets**,
   - the sequence with **Sequence**,
   - the tempo with **Tempo** (empty means: as in the song).

**Reset the band arrangement to the song** removes the arrangement again. In the
song list, a band icon next to a song shows that the band has an arrangement for it.

## The key in the set

Each song of the set shows its key on the right. Tap it and pick one of the twelve
keys (**Key in this set – for the whole band**). If the band has an arrangement, its
key is the starting point: "−2" means two semitones below the band's key.

## The song sequence in the set

The song sequence says in which order the parts of the song are played, e.g.
`I, V1, C, V2, C x2, B, O` (intro, verse 1, chorus, verse 2, chorus twice, bridge,
outro).

1. In the song's ⋮ menu, tap **Song sequence for this set**.
2. Under **Parts of the song**, tap the parts in the order you play them, or drag
   them into the sequence. Long-press a part to move it; **Clear** starts over.
3. **Edit as text** lets you type the sequence directly.

<Image src={sequence} alt="Building the song sequence for a set from the parts of the song" />

**Use the song's sequence** removes a set sequence again. In live mode, an icon next
to the sequence shows where it comes from: **Sequence changed for this set** or
**Sequence of the band**.

In live mode, setDING shows the whole song in its own order; the parts that do not
appear in the sequence are dimmed. If you would rather not see them, switch off
**Show parts outside the sequence** in the display settings: setDING then shows only
the parts of the sequence and names the hidden parts in the song's header. Opening
the song on its own always shows all of it.

## PDF or chords in live mode

If a song has PDF attachments, an icon next to the key shows what you see in live
mode: the ChordPro text or one of the PDFs. Tap it to switch. This choice is yours
alone and applies in all sets.
