A set is the songs of a gig or a rehearsal in the right order. Every set belongs to
a band; all members of the band see it on their device.

The band's **Leader** and **Set planner** and the admins of the organization may
create and change sets. Everybody else sees the set and plays it, but does not
change it (see [Roles](/en/bands/roles/)).

<Image src={set} alt="A set with two songs, their keys, a break and the offline-ready status" />

## The list of sets

**Sets** shows the sets of the selected band. If you are in several bands, switch
between them at the top right with **Switch band**.

The sets are grouped by date: **Upcoming**, **Without date** and **Past**. Past sets
are collapsed; tap **Show past sets** to see them. The next set is marked as
**Next gig**, with the number of days until then.

Pull the list down to sync right away.

<Image src={sets} alt="The list of sets with the next gig beside it" />

## Create a new set

1. On **Sets**, tap **New set**.
2. Enter a **Name**, optionally the **Venue**, and the date of the gig with **Pick
   date**.
3. Tap **Save**.

Change the name, venue and date later with the pencil **Edit set** at the top of the set.

## Add songs

1. Open the set and tap **Add songs**.
2. Select the songs; **Search songs** finds them faster.
3. Tap **Add**. The songs are added at the end of the set.

To change the order, drag an item up or down by the handle on the right.

## Markers: breaks, announcements, encore …

**Add marker** in the set's ⋮ menu adds a marker at the end of the set, for example
a break, an announcement, the encore, a change of instrument or a light effect:

1. Choose an **icon**. The icons are grouped by topic: break, announcement, flow,
   stage, show and general.
2. The **Text** field takes the icon's name, e.g. "Encore". Keep it or overwrite it,
   e.g. with "Encore: Last Call". The text has at most 24 characters.
3. The **preview** shows how the marker looks in live mode. Tap **Save**.

<Image src={markerDialog} alt="The Add marker dialog with the icons grouped by topic, the text Encore and the preview" />

If you keep the icon's name, every band member sees it in their own language:
"Encore" becomes "Zugabe" in German. Then drag the marker to the right place.

Markers show with their icon in the set, as a page of their own in live mode and as
separator rows in the printed set list. Tap a marker to change its icon and text.

## Remove items

In an item's ⋮ menu, tap **Remove from set**. The song stays in the song library;
it is only removed from this set. Right afterwards, **Undo** brings it back.

## Copy and delete a set

- **Copy to band** copies the set with all its items into another band of the same
  organization, e.g. for a second line-up. There the other band's arrangements
  apply (see [Key and sequence](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/)).
- **Delete** deletes the set. The songs are kept.

## Trash

Deleted sets can be restored with all their items for **30 days**: the set list's
⋮ menu → **Trash** → **Restore**. Whoever may manage sets sees the trash. After 30
days a deleted set is removed for good.
