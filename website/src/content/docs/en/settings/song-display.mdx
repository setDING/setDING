Under **Settings → Song display** you choose how the sections of a song look in
**live mode** and in the **song view**: for example the chorus bold with a wide bar,
the bridge in italics or the chords of the solo in amber. The setting is
**personal** and applies on all your devices. The song itself stays unchanged for
everybody, and the preview in the editor always shows it without your styles.

<Image src={shot} alt="The “Song display” settings with the preview, the presets and the sections" />

## Presets

Here you pick a **preset**; the **preview** below shows a song in the look of your
stage:

- **Classic** – as before: bold chords, a thin bar next to the chorus.
- **Colour-coded** – every section has its colour for bar, heading and chords.
- **Chorus focus** – the chorus bold, with a wide bar and a tinted background; the
  bridge in italics.
- **Vocals** – the lyrics in front, chords small and grey.
- **Guitar** – large chords, the lyrics toned down; bridge and solo stand out.
- **Minimal** – no bars, only the heading of the chorus.

As soon as you change something it becomes **Custom**. If you pick a preset again
afterwards, setDING asks whether to discard your custom styles.

## Customise

**Customise** opens the editor. On a tablet the settings are on the left and the
preview stays next to them; on a phone the preview is pinned on top, and you drag
the handle below it to make it larger or smaller. When you pick a section, the
preview jumps to it.

At the top right of the preview you choose the song: the **demo song**, which has
every section once, or a **song from the library**. The choice applies to this
device only.

## Sections

setDING recognises the sections by their name, from `{start_of_…}` or from a
heading written as a comment such as `{c: Solo}` (see
[ChordPro](/en/songs/chordpro/)):

| Section | Recognised from |
|---|---|
| Intro | Intro |
| Verse | Verse, Vers, Strophe |
| Pre-chorus | Pre-Chorus, Pre-Refrain |
| Chorus | Chorus, Refrain |
| Bridge | Bridge |
| Interlude | Interlude, Instrumental, Zwischenspiel, Solo, Turnaround, Vamp |
| Outro | Outro, Ending, Schluss, Coda, Tag |

**Standard** is the base style: it applies to every section without its own style
and to everything that is not recognised, e.g. tabs, chord grids or custom names.

## Own style or take it over

Every section either has its **own style** or takes over the style of another
section (**Like …**). So you can set up just the interlude, and intro and outro look
the same. At the start, intro and outro take over the interlude, the pre-chorus the
verse and all others the standard. A ring instead of a dot in the list of sections
shows that a section takes over its style.

A section cannot take over from a section that already takes over from it; such
entries are greyed out.

## What you can set

- **Chords** – bold, italic, size (S, M, L) and colour.
- **Lyrics** – bold, italic and colour.
- **Heading** – shown or not, bold, italic, size (S, M, L, XL) and colour.
- **Indent bar** – shown or not, width and colour; plus the **indent** and a
  **tinted background**.

The colours come from a fixed palette that stays readable on light, dark and black
backgrounds. **Automatic** (A) keeps the usual colour; **Black/White** is black
in the light theme and white in the dark one.

## Formatting in the song

Some songs bring their own formatting:

- A colour you **choose explicitly** wins over a colour from the song (e.g.
  `{chordcolour: red}`). With **Automatic**, the song's colour applies.
- **Sizes** add up: large chords in your style and `{chordsize: 120%}` in the song
  give even larger chords.
- **Emphasis in the text** such as `<b>quietly!</b>` always applies.
