In **Settings** you choose how setDING shows songs to you. The song itself stays
unchanged for everybody.

The settings under **General**, **Musician** and **Display** are **personal**: they
belong to your account and apply on all your devices. **Screen & live mode** and
**Storage & info** apply only on the device where you set them – a phone needs a
different font size than a tablet.

## Display

- **Chord notation** – **International (B, Bb)** or **German (H, B)**. In German
  notation, the chord called B internationally is H, and Bb is B.
- **Show Nashville numbers** – chords as scale degrees of the key, e.g. 1, 4, 5, 6m.
  Handy when the key changes often. Needs a key in the song. You can also switch
  single songs in the [personal adjustments](/en/songs/personal/) or in live mode.
- **Chord style** – how chords are spelled:
  - **As written** – the way they are in the song,
  - **Standard** – e.g. Am7, Cmaj7, Em7b5,
  - **Jazz** – e.g. A−7, CΔ7, Eø7.
- **Raise extensions** – extensions smaller and raised, e.g. C⁷. Needs the
  **Standard** or **Jazz** style.
- **Content** – **Lyrics and chords** or **Lyrics only**, e.g. for singers.
- **Repeated chorus** – a chorus that is repeated: **Show in full**, **Label only**,
  or **Automatic**: as a short label only if the song would otherwise not fit well on
  the screen.
- **Progress bar of the set** – the thin bar in live mode.

## Musician

- **My instrument** – **Guitar**, **Ukulele**, **Bass**, **Keyboard**, **Vocals**,
  **Drums** or **Other …**. Songs can contain parts for one instrument only, e.g. a
  guitar solo as a tab; you see the parts for your instrument. The instrument also
  decides the chord diagrams.
- **My name or voice** – for parts for one voice or person only, e.g. "alto" or
  "tenor".
- **Chord diagrams** – shows the fingerings of all chords of the song for your
  instrument, at the **Top** or **Bottom**, or **Off**.
- **Beat and click** – see [Beat, click and drums](/en/live/beat/).

## Screen & live mode

These settings apply to this device only:

- **Font size** – the base font. Make single songs larger or smaller in the personal
  adjustments or in live mode.
- **Fit song to screen** – the whole song fits on the screen without scrolling, in
  columns and with text as large as possible. Switched off, the text keeps its size
  and you scroll.
- **Columns** – the highest number of columns when fitting: **Automatic**, **One**,
  **Two** or **Three**.
- **Page turn** – **Full page** or **Half page**: with half-page turns, the top half
  already shows the next page while you are still playing the bottom half.
- **Appearance on stage** – **As app**, **Dark** or **Black**, independent of the app
  theme. **Black** saves power on OLED screens.
