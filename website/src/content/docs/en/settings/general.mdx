## General

- **Language** – in the drop-down **System language**, **Deutsch**, **English**,
  **Français**, **Italiano**, **Español** or **Português**. With **System
  language**, setDING follows your device's language, otherwise English. Deutsch
  is Swiss Standard German. The names of song parts (Verse, Chorus, …) stay
  English in every language.
- **Theme** – **As system**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Accent colour** – the colour of buttons and highlights, e.g. **setDING blue**,
  **Green** or **Purple**.
- **Close brackets and sections automatically** – in the
  [ChordPro editor](/en/songs/editor/#closing-brackets-and-sections), `[` adds a `]`,
  **Enter** after a section start adds its end, and a renamed start renames its end
  too. On by default.

These settings are personal and apply on all devices you are signed in on. While
signed out, they apply on this device only; after signing in, they are synced with
your account.

On a wide screen the settings use the full width and spread their sections over
two columns; the song display stays in one column, so its preview gets the whole
width.

## Storage & info

### Backup

- **Back up everything** saves one file with every library you may back up (*On
  this device* and the organizations where you are a librarian or admin) with
  songs, sets and attachments, plus your personal adjustments and notes from all
  other organizations and your settings. Below it you see when you last backed up
  the library *On this device*. If it changed and was not backed up for more than
  30 days, setDING reminds you at start (at most once a week, never in live mode).
- **Restore backup** imports such a file as a copy: you choose where each library
  goes. Your personal adjustments come back on the songs that are still here.
  setDING takes over your settings only if you tick it. The library *On this
  device* of the backup can also **replace** the current one, with a safety backup
  first, see [Replace the local library](/en/songs/library/#replace-the-local-library).
  See also
  [Back up the library](/en/songs/library/#back-up-the-library).
### This device

- **PDF attachments use …** shows how much space the PDF sheet music takes on this
  device.
- **Show the local library** (not in the web app): if you play only in
  organizations, switch it off to hide *On this device* from the lists of
  libraries and bands, the overview, the song and set lists, imports and copying.
  New songs then go to the current organization. Its songs and sets stay on this
  device and come back when you switch it on again; the full and the automatic
  backup still include it, and setDING no longer reminds you to back it up. You
  can switch it off only while signed in; signed out, the local library is always
  shown.

### Automatic backup

- **Back up automatically** (not in the web app) saves the library *On this device*
  into a folder you choose, at most once a day and only after it changed. Choose
  a folder of your cloud storage, so the library is safe when the device is lost:
  on Android a folder of any app that offers its storage to the system (e.g.
  Nextcloud, OneDrive, Dropbox), on iPad and iPhone a folder in the Files app
  (e.g. iCloud Drive), on Windows and Linux any folder, e.g. one the OneDrive,
  Dropbox or Nextcloud client syncs. setDING checks at start and when you leave
  the app, never in live mode. While the local library is hidden, you see this
  section only as long as the automatic backup is still on, so you can switch it
  off.
  - **Folder** shows where the backups go: the storage or the app (e.g.
    «Internal shared storage», «Nextcloud») and the path, on Windows and Linux
    the whole path. If it is too long, setDING shortens the folders in the
    middle with «…».
  - The files are called `Backup <device> <date> <time>.setDING.zip`. Change the
    **Device name** if several devices write into the same folder.
  - **Backups kept** (1 to 10) says how many of this device's automatic backups
    stay; older ones of this device are deleted, other files never.
  - **Back up to the folder now** writes one at once. Below it you see the last automatic
    backup, or why the last attempt failed. If the folder cannot be reached or
    setDING lost the permission, a hint at start offers to choose it again.
  - While automatic backups succeed, setDING does not remind you of a backup.
  - Restore such a file with **Restore backup**, as a copy or by
    [replacing the local library](/en/songs/library/#replace-the-local-library).

### Help & info

- **User guide** opens this documentation in the browser, at the first steps.
- **Copy diagnostic log** puts the app's recent messages on the clipboard, e.g. why
  a sync failed. Paste them into a message to whoever runs your server, or into an
  [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/setDING/setDING/issues).
- At the very bottom is the **Version** of setDING.

The **book icon** at the top of every settings category opens the page of this
documentation that describes it. You also find it where a term needs more than a
sentence: at the members of a band and the people of an organization (the
[roles](/en/bands/roles/)), in the [ChordPro editor](/en/songs/editor/) and in a
band's arrangement ([key and sequence](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/)). The pages open
in the language of the app, or in English if this documentation does not have it.
