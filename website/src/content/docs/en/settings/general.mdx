## General

- **Language** – **System language**, **Deutsch** or **English**. With **System
  language**, setDING follows your device's language. Deutsch is Swiss Standard
  German.
- **Theme** – **As system**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Accent colour** – the colour of buttons and highlights, e.g. **setDING blue**,
  **Green** or **Purple**.
- **Close brackets and sections automatically** – in the
  [ChordPro editor](/en/songs/editor/#closing-brackets-and-sections), `[` adds a `]`,
  **Enter** after a section start adds its end, and a renamed start renames its end
  too. On by default.

These settings are personal and apply on all devices you are signed in on. While
signed out, they apply on this device only; after signing in, they are synced with
your account.

On a wide screen the settings use the full width and spread their sections over
two columns; the song display stays in one column, so its preview gets the whole
width.

## Storage & info

- **PDF attachments use …** shows how much space the PDF sheet music takes on this
  device.
- **Back up everything** saves one file with every library you may back up (*On
  this device* and the organizations where you are a librarian or admin) with
  songs, sets and attachments, plus your personal adjustments and notes from all
  other organizations and your settings. Below it you see when you last backed up
  the library *On this device*. If it changed and was not backed up for more than
  30 days, setDING reminds you at start (at most once a week, never in live mode).
- **Restore backup** imports such a file as a copy: you choose where each library
  goes. Your personal adjustments come back on the songs that are still here.
  setDING takes over your settings only if you tick it. See also
  [Back up the library](/en/songs/library/#back-up-the-library).
- **Copy diagnostic log** puts the app's recent messages on the clipboard, e.g. why
  a sync failed. Paste them into a message to whoever runs your server, or into an
  [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tafli/setDING/issues).
- At the very bottom is the **Version** of setDING.
