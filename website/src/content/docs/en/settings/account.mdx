setDING works without an account too, but only on your device. With an account you
share songs and sets with your band, and your personal adjustments and settings are
on all your devices.

## Sign in and create an account

1. In **Settings** → **Account & sync**, tap **Sign in**.
2. Sign in with your **Email** and **Password**, or tap **No account yet? Create
   one**. Your **Name** is how your bandmates see you.
3. If the server asks for a confirmation, you get an email. Confirm your address with
   the link in it, then sign in.

If you received an invitation, open the invitation link first or tap **Join with
invitation** (see [Invitations](/en/bands/invitations/)). Many servers only accept
new accounts with an invitation.

**Forgot password?** sends you an email to reset it.

You stay signed in, even if you are offline for a long time.

## Sync

Everything you change is saved on your device first and then sent to the server.
Under **Sync** you choose when:

- **Automatic** – at start, a few seconds after changes, when something changes on
  the server, and every 5 minutes.
- **At start** – once when the app starts and after signing in.
- **Manually** – only when you tap **Sync now**.

**Sync** shows when the last sync was, whether changes are still waiting to go to the
server and whether PDFs are still being downloaded. **Sync now** syncs right away;
so does pulling down the list on **Sets** or **Songs**.

Without a connection you keep working as usual. setDING says **No connection –
changes will be sent later.** and catches up as soon as you are back online.

## Changes by others

When someone else changes or deletes the song or set you have open, setDING tells
you after the sync, for example **"Morning Light" was changed by Anna.** A deleted
song or set closes. Your own changes, also from your other devices, tell nothing.

<Image src={syncHint} alt="A song with the hint: “Morning Light” was changed by Anna." />

In live mode nothing changes on stage: setDING syncs afterwards. One hint then sums up
what others changed in the meantime, for example **While you were playing live: 1
song deleted, 2 songs changed.** **Show** opens the recent changes of the overview.

## Update setDING

If your version is too old for the server, **Please update setDING** appears. Your
changes stay on the device and are sent after the update. How to update is described
in [Installation](/en/start/install/).

## Change your name and password

When you are signed in, **Settings** → **Account & sync** offers:

- **Change name** – how your bandmates see you. They see the new name after their
  next sync.
- **Change password** – enter your **Current password** and the **New password**
  twice (at least 8 characters). You stay signed in on this device; your other
  devices sign out, and you sign in there with the new password.

Both need a connection to the server.

## Sign out

**Sign out** ends the sign-in on this device. Songs and sets on the device are kept.

Changing the **Server** field signs you out.

## Delete your account

**Delete account** is at the very end of **Account & sync**, in the red **Danger zone** (in the picture above). Enter your password to
confirm. This deletes for good:

- your account (name, email address, password),
- your personal adjustments, annotations and settings,
- your memberships in organizations and bands.

Songs and sets you created or edited stay with your organizations. You are signed
out on all devices; this device removes the data of your organizations and keeps
your local library. The server's backups keep a copy for up to 14 days. Deleting
needs a connection to the server and cannot be undone.

If you are the **only admin** of an organization, first make someone else admin or
close the organization (see [Organization](/en/bands/organizations/)).
