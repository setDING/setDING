This privacy policy applies to the website `setding.app` and to the setDING server
at `api.setding.app` and `app.setding.app`, which the app connects to by default.
It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies,
the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Last updated: 26 November 2026

## Controller

info@setDING.app

See also the [legal notice](/en/legal-notice/).

## In short

- setDING is free and open source. There are **no ads, no tracking and no
  analytics tools**, neither in the app nor on the website.
- Without an account, everything stays on your device. Data only goes to the server
  when you sign in.
- The server is in Switzerland. Data is not sold and not used for any purpose other
  than running setDING.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time.

## Without an account: data on your device

The app stores songs, sets, attachments, annotations and settings in a local
database on your device. This data does not leave the device as long as you are not
signed in. If you turn on the **automatic backup**, the app writes backups to the
folder you choose yourself. If that folder is at a cloud provider, the provider's
privacy policy applies.

At most once a day, the installed app asks the configured server for the newest
version to show you updates. No identifier of your device is sent; the server only
sees the technical data listed under [Server logs](#server-logs).

## With an account: data on the server

When you sign in, the app syncs with the server. It stores:

- **Account:** first name, last name, nickname, email address, password (only as a
  hash) and when the account was created.
- **Content of your organizations and bands:** songs, PDF attachments,
  arrangements, sets, lineups, invitations and who changed what and when.
- **People without an account** whom admins add to a lineup: name, nickname and
  optionally an email address for a personal invitation. Whoever adds such details
  makes sure the person agrees.
- **Personal data:** your adjustments to songs, annotations and your synced
  settings.
- **Play history:** which songs were played in past sets.

Only the members of your organizations and bands see this data, according to their
[role](/en/bands/roles/). Its only purpose is that your band has its songs and sets
on every device. The legal basis is providing the service you use with your account
(Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Emails

The server only sends emails your account needs: confirming your email address,
resetting your password and invitations. We use cyon AG,
Switzerland, for this. There is no newsletter.

## Server logs

When you open the website, the web client or the server, technical data is
recorded: IP address, time, requested address, status code and browser or app
version. It serves secure operation and troubleshooting and is deleted after
7 days, and from the server's backups after another 14 days at the latest. The
website itself keeps no logs.

## Where the data is

- **Hosting:** Infomaniak Network SA, Geneva, Switzerland. Servers and backups are
  in Switzerland.
- **Cloudflare:** connections to the website and the server go through Cloudflare,
  Inc. (USA), which secures and forwards them. Cloudflare may process the
  transmitted data, also outside Switzerland. Cloudflare is certified under the
  Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## How long

The data is kept as long as your account exists. Deleted bands and organizations
stay in the trash for 30 days and are then removed for good. The server's backups
keep a copy for up to 14 days.

## Deleting your account

In the app, you delete your account under **Settings** → **Account & sync** →
**Delete account** (see [Account](/en/settings/account/)). If you no longer have access to
the app, write to the address under [Controller](#controller); we then delete the
account within 30 days.

Deleted are your account, your personal adjustments, annotations and settings and
your memberships. Songs and sets you created or edited belong to the organization
and stay with it.

## Your rights

You can ask for information about your data and have it corrected, deleted or
handed over, and you can object to its processing. Write to the address under
[Controller](#controller). You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data
Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or to the supervisory authority of
your country.

## Your own server

If the app connects to another server (see
[Self-hosting](/en/self-hosting/overview/)), its operator is responsible for the
data. This policy then only applies to the app itself.
