## The terms

- **Organization:** for example a music school, a church or simply your band. All
  songs belong to the organization, and all its bands share them.
- **Band:** a group of people from the organization who play together. Each band
  has its own sets and chooses its own key, sequence and tempo for every song.
- **Set:** the songs of a gig or rehearsal in the right order, with breaks and
  notes.
- **Song:** a chord sheet in the [ChordPro](https://www.chordpro.org/) format,
  optionally with PDF sheet music.

## Roles

In an **organization** there are admins, librarians (they look after the songs) and
members. In a **band** there are band leaders, set planners (they put sets together)
and members. Every role may do everything the roles below it may do; organization
admins may do everything. More in [Roles](/en/bands/roles/).

## Joining a band

1. [Install setDING](/en/start/install/).
2. Open the invitation link you got from the band leader or an admin, or scan the QR
   code. You can also paste the link in the app under **Join**.
3. Sign in or create an account.
4. Tap **Join**. You then see the organization's songs and your band's sets.

Invitation links are valid for 7 days. If a link has expired, ask for a new one.

## The overview

When it starts, setDING shows your band's **Overview**:

<Image src={overview} alt="The overview with the next set, Ready to play, more sets, the newest songs and the songs not played for a while" />

- **Next set:** the next gig, with **Start live** and **Open set**.
- **Ready to play:** whether this device is ready to play (see below).
- **More sets:** the next three gigs after it.
- **To do** and **Recent changes**, when there is something to fix or others
  changed something.
- **Newest songs:** the five songs added to the library last, your own ones too.
- **Not played for a while:** songs your band has not played for a long time,
  handy for the next rehearsal. **Show all** opens the
  [repertoire](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/#the-bands-repertoire).

Tap an entry to open the song or set. setDING leaves out a card with nothing to
show.

At the very bottom of the screen are the version, **What's new** and, when there
is a newer version, the hint to update.

### Ready to play

**Ready to play** shows whether this device is ready for the next gig. When
everything is fine, it only says **All set**. Whatever you should fix before the
gig is always shown, marked in orange. **Show details** shows every point:

<Image src={readiness} alt="Ready to play with details: musician, MIDI keyboard not connected, next set available offline and the page turner" />

- **Musician:** your instrument and your name.
- **MIDI:** only for keyboard players with MIDI program changes on: whether your
  keyboard is connected and which profile applies.
- **Next set offline:** whether all songs and PDFs of the next gig are on this
  device.
- **Sync:** when this device last synced and whether changes have not reached
  the server yet (only when signed in).
- **Page turner:** when it was last used and whether it turns full or half
  pages. Press the pedal on the overview: if it works, it says **Page turner
  responds**. That way you test it before the gig without opening a set. Once you
  [paired](/en/live/play/#with-a-foot-pedal) your pedal, the line shows whether
  it is connected.
- **Automatic backup:** when the last one was made or why it failed (only when
  switched on).

Tap a point to go straight to its setting, or to the set for the next set.

## With a keyboard

On a computer or a tablet with a keyboard, many things take a single key. The **?**
key shows you all keyboard shortcuts at any time:

<Image src={shortcuts} alt="The Keyboard shortcuts dialog: Esc, ?, E, L, + and −, arrow keys, search and the keys of live mode" />

- **Esc** takes you one step back on every screen, like the arrow at the top left,
  e.g. from a song back to the song list. If a dialog or menu is open, Esc first
  closes only that.
- **E** edits the song or set if you may; **L** starts live mode.
- **+** and **−** transpose the song for you (like the key under **Personal
  adjustments**).
- **←** and **→** go to the previous and next song when you opened the song from a
  set.
- **/** or **Ctrl+F** (Mac: **⌘F**) jumps to the search of the song list.
- With songs selected in the song list, **Esc** closes the selection, like the **X**
  at the top left.

While you type in a text field, the letter shortcuts do not apply: an "e" stays an
"e".

## Playing offline

setDING keeps everything on your device. Once all songs and PDFs of a set are there,
you no longer need a network for the gig; if something is missing, setDING marks the
set. Changes you make offline are synced as soon as you
are back online.
