## Why setDING?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Made for the stage" icon="rocket">
    Live mode in full screen; the screen stays on for the whole gig. Turn pages with
    a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth foot pedal – your hands stay on your instrument.
    Your keyboard switches to the right sound for every song over MIDI. Works
    completely offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sets the whole band shares" icon="list-format">
    Drag and drop songs into a set, add breaks and notes, set the key and the
    sequence per song. Every member gets the set on their device, and setDING shows
    when something is still missing for a gig without a network.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The band plays together" icon="puzzle">

    Spot a mistake in rehearsal? Report it right on the song.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for drummers too" icon="seti:audio">

## Made for bands, not just for one player

Many chord apps are a personal songbook you can share. setDING thinks of the whole
band: everyone who rehearses, plays the gig, fills in and looks after the songs.

- **A lineup per set** – who plays this gig, with substitutes from other bands of
  the organization, and a message to everyone in the lineup.
- **The band's arrangement** – key, sequence and tempo per band, without changing
  the song for the other bands.
- **Last played** – the key, tempo and sequence of every gig are
  kept, along with the repertoire: most played, not played for a while, never
  played.
- **Report a mistake** – everyone in the organization reports a wrong chord right on
  the song; whoever looks after the library sees and resolves the reports.
- **Review songs** – imported songs stay marked until someone has checked and
  approved them.
