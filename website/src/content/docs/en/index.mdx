## Why setDING?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Made for the stage" icon="rocket">
    <Image src={why1} alt="Live mode: a song in three columns, half light, half dark" />

    Full-screen live mode; turn pages with a tap, a swipe or a foot pedal. Your
    keyboard switches sounds over MIDI. Works completely offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sets the whole band shares" icon="list-format">
    <Image src={why2} alt="A set with six songs, a break, and a key and sequence per song" />

    Drag and drop songs into a set, set the key and the sequence per song – and
    everyone in the band has it on their device.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your songs, your format" icon="document">
    <Image src={why3} alt="The ChordPro editor with its live preview" />

    Songs are [ChordPro](https://www.chordpro.org/), the open standard for chord
    sheets. Import one by one or as a ZIP, edit with a live preview.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every musician sees it their way" icon="setting">
    <Image src={why4} alt="Customizing the song display: font, sections and chords with a preview" />

    Transpose, capo, font size, Nashville numbers, cues for your instrument –
    without changing the song for the rest of the band.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ready for the gig" icon="approve-check-circle">
    <Image src={why5} alt="The overview with the next set and the stage check" />

    The overview shows the next set, and the stage check tells you whether
    anything is still missing for a gig without a network.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For bands, schools and churches" icon="star">
    <Image src={why6} alt="The song list with the key, tempo and sequence of every song" />

    Several bands of an organization share one song library. Invite with a link
    or a QR code; roles decide who may do what.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for drummers too" icon="seti:audio">
    <Image src={why7} alt="Drummer mode with tempo, time signature and song sequence" />

    Tempo, time signature and song sequence instead of chords, a count-in and a
    tight click on your in-ears.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your data stays yours" icon="approve-check">
    <Image src={why8} alt="The settings with the address of your own server" />

    Run the server yourself, on a VPS, a NAS or at home –
    [here's how](/en/self-hosting/overview/). Open source under the MIT licence.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Made for bands, not just for solo musicians

Many chord apps are a personal songbook you can share. setDING thinks of the whole
band: everyone who rehearses, plays the gig, fills in and looks after the songs.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A lineup for every set" icon="puzzle">
    <div class="screens">
      <Image src={lineupDialog} alt="The Lineup dialog with Anna, Beat and Carla as a substitute" />
      <Image src={lineupNotify} alt="The message to everyone in the lineup with date, place and lineup" />
    </div>

    Who plays this gig, substitutes from other bands of the organization
    included – and a message to everyone in the lineup.
    [Lineup and substitutes](/en/sets/lineup/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="The band's arrangement" icon="random">
    <div class="screens">
      <Image src={sequence} alt="The sequence of a song for this set: verse, chorus, bridge" />
    </div>

    Key, sequence and tempo per band, without changing the song for the other
    bands. [Key and sequence](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Last played" icon="clock">
    <div class="screens">
      <Image src={songsPlayed} alt="The song list with the Played filter: never played, not played for a while" />
    </div>

    The key, tempo and sequence of every gig are kept, together with the
    repertoire: most played, not played for a while, never played.
    [How did we play it last time?](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/#how-did-we-play-it-last-time)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Report mistakes, review songs" icon="comment-alt">
    <div class="screens">
      <Image src={songReport} alt="Reporting a mistake on a song: kind of mistake and a comment" />
      <Image src={songsReview} alt="The song list with the To review and Reported filters" />
    </div>

    Anyone can report a wrong chord right on the song; whoever looks after the
    library resolves the reports. Imported songs stay marked until someone
    approves them. [Review songs and report mistakes](/en/songs/review/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
