## Why setDING?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Made for the stage" icon="rocket">
    Live mode in full screen; the screen stays on for the whole gig. Turn pages with
    a tap, a swipe or a Bluetooth foot pedal – your hands stay on your instrument.
    Works completely offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sets the whole band shares" icon="list-format">
    Drag and drop songs into a set, add breaks and notes, set the key and the
    sequence per song. Every member gets the set on their device, and setDING shows
    when it is ready to play offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for drummers too" icon="seti:audio">
    Tempo, time signature and song sequence instead of chords, a count-in and a
    tight click on your in-ears – a mode just for drums.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every musician sees it their way" icon="setting">
    Transpose with one tap, set a capo, change the font size, use B or H notation or
    Nashville numbers. Personal notes never change the song for the rest of the band.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your songs, your format" icon="document">
    Songs are [ChordPro](https://www.chordpro.org/), the open standard for chord
    sheets. Import them one by one or as a ZIP, attach PDF sheet music, edit them in
    the built-in editor with a live preview.
  </Card>
  <Card title="For bands, schools and churches" icon="star">
    Several bands of an organization share one song library. Invite members with a
    link or a QR code. Roles decide who looks after the songs and who plans sets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your data stays yours" icon="approve-check">
    setDING syncs with a self-hosted server. Open source under the MIT licence.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Finally, something for the drummer

Chord apps think of guitar, keys and vocals. setDING has a live mode of its own for
drums: instead of the chord sheet, the **song map** shows the parts of the song in
the band's sequence, each with a cue from the lyrics and your drum notes. Beside it,
the tempo and time signature in large type, the beats lighting up, and the whole set
one tap away.

- **Count-in and click** – one or two bars of count-in and a click timed by the
  audio output, not by a timer. With a measured delay, Bluetooth in-ears work too.
- **Hands on the kit** – start the beat with the foot pedal (long press) or
  automatically for every song; the edge of the screen flashes on the beat.
- **Tempo on stage** – faster, slower or tap tempo, just for tonight.
- **Odd meters** – 6/8 in the felt pulse, 7/8 accented as 2+2+3.
- **Notes just for you** – only those who play drums see `{comment-drums: …}`.
