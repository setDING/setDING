## Made for bands, not just for solo musicians

Many chord apps are a personal songbook you can share. setDING thinks of the whole
band: everyone who rehearses, plays the gig, fills in and looks after the songs.

- **A lineup per set** – who plays this gig, with substitutes from other bands of
  the organization, and a message to everyone in the lineup.
- **The band's arrangement** – key, sequence and tempo per band, without changing
  the song for the other bands.
- **Last played** – the key, tempo and sequence of every gig are
  kept, along with the repertoire: most played, not played for a while, never
  played.
- **Report a mistake** – everyone in the organization reports a wrong chord right on
  the song; whoever looks after the library sees and resolves the reports.
- **Review songs** – imported songs stay marked until someone has checked and
  approved them.
