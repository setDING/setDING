New people join the organization with an invitation link – by group chat, email or
QR code.

## Invite

There are two kinds of invitations:

- **Into the organization:** admins of the organization open the organization and
  tap **Invite people**. Whoever opens the link joins the organization with the
  role **Member**, but no band yet: an admin then adds them to a band (**Add
  member**) and can change their [role](/en/bands/roles/) under **People**, for
  example to **Librarian** or **Admin**.
- **Straight into a band:** the band's **Leader** and the admins open the band and
  tap **Invite people** there. Whoever opens the link joins the organization and
  this band with the role **Member**. Under **Members** the leader can then change
  their role in the band, for example to **Set planner** or **Leader**.

setDING shows the **Invitation link** and a QR code. Share the link with **Copy
link** or let people scan the QR code. The link is valid for **7 days**; several
people can use the same link.

## Manage invitations

**Invitation links** lists the valid links of the organization or the band: which
band they lead into (or **Organization only**), how long they are valid and who
joined with them. Tap a link or its QR code icon to **show it again** with its QR
code. **Revoke** stops a link at once; people who already joined stay members.
Revoked and expired links disappear from the list.

## Follow an invitation

1. [Install setDING](/en/start/install/).
2. Open the invitation link or scan the QR code. If the app does not open by itself,
   copy the link and paste it in the app: in **Settings** → **Account & sync** →
   **Join with invitation**, or, if you are already signed in, in **Organization &
   bands** → **Join**.
3. Sign in, or create an account if you do not have one yet.
4. Confirm with **Join**.

You then see the songs of the organization and, for an invitation into a band, its
sets too.

If setDING says **This invitation has expired** or **This invitation was revoked**,
ask for a new link.

Many servers only accept new accounts with an invitation link. Then open the link
first and create the account after that.
