Every person has one role in the organization and one in each of their bands. The
roles are ordered: each may do everything the roles below it may. Admins of the
organization may do everything, in every one of its bands too.

## In the organization

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything: people and their roles, bands, invitations, the song library, the sets of all bands |
| **Librarian** | Create, import, edit and delete the organization's songs and PDF attachments |
| **Member** | See the organization's people and songs, adjust songs for themselves |

Admins change roles in the organization: open the organization and choose **Make
admin**, **Make librarian** or **Make member** in the person's ⋮ menu.

## In the band

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Leader** | Everything in the band: sets, band arrangements, members and their roles, invitations into the band. Also create and import new songs and edit their own |
| **Set planner** | Create, change and delete the band's sets and band arrangements |
| **Member** | See the band's sets and play them live |

The leader and the admins of the organization change roles in the band: open the
band and choose **Make leader**, **Make set planner** or **Make member** in the
person's ⋮ menu.

## Typical line-ups

- **A single band:** whoever founds the organization is its admin and does
  everything. Someone who helps keep the songs gets **Librarian**.
- **Music school or church:** the head of the school or church is the admin. One
  person keeps the songs for everybody as **Librarian**. Every band has its
  **Leader**, who plans the sets, often with a deputy as **Set planner**.

setDING does not even show what a role may not do: members see neither **New set**
nor **Edit ChordPro**, for example. Everybody can adjust everything for themselves
(see [Personal adjustments](/en/songs/personal/)).

Someone who only helps out at some gigs does not join the band: they are added to
the lineup of those sets as a **Substitute** and see only them (see
[Lineup and substitutes](/en/sets/lineup/)).
