An **organization** is, for example, a music school, a church or simply your band.
It owns the songs and the people. A **band** is a group of people from the
organization who play together; every band has its own sets and band arrangements.
All bands of an organization share the same song library.

Organizations and bands need an account: **Settings** → **Account & sync** → **Sign
in** (where you can also **Create account**). Without an account, setDING works on
your device only, in the band **On this device**.

## Manage bands

Everything about your organizations and bands is in **Settings** → **Account &
sync** → **Manage bands**, or on **Sets** in the **Switch band** menu. The list shows
your organizations with their bands; tap an organization or band to open it.

## Found an organization

The operator of your server decides how new organizations come about:

- **Invitation only** (usual): the server operator sends you a founder invitation.
  Open the link, sign in or create an account and tap **Found organization**. You
  become the admin of the new organization.
- **Open:** in **Manage bands**, tap **New band** and choose **New organization** as
  the **Organization**.

## Create a band

Admins of the organization tap **New band** in **Manage bands**, enter a **Band
name** and choose the **Organization**.

## The organization

In the organization, everybody sees the **Bands** and the **People**. Admins can
also:

- change the **Organization name** and create more bands,
- change everybody's role (see [Roles](/en/bands/roles/)),
- invite people (see [Invitations](/en/bands/invitations/)),
- **Remove from organization**. The person then also leaves all bands of the
  organization.

**Leave organization** takes you out yourself; you then also leave all its bands and
no longer see its songs.

## The band

The band shows its **Members**. The band's **Leader** and the admins of the
organization can:

- change the **Band name**,
- take people of the organization into the band with **Add member**,
- invite new people straight into the band,
- change roles in the band and **Remove from band**.

**Leave band** takes you out yourself. A band always needs at least one leader.

## Switch between bands

If you are in several bands, choose the band you work with on **Sets** at the top
with **Switch band**: you then see its sets, and songs show its band arrangement. On
**Songs**, **Switch song library** changes the organization.

**Copy to band** takes a set or songs into another band or organization.
