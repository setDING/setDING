You can draw on songs and PDF sheet music in live mode: circle a chord, draw an
arrow from the verse to the chorus, highlight a line or put a fermata where the band
holds. Your annotations are **yours alone** – nobody else in the band sees them –
and they are synced with your account to all your devices.

## Start drawing

Tap the middle of the screen to show the control bar and tap the pen
(**Draw annotations**). The tools appear at the bottom of the screen. **Done** or the
Escape key ends drawing mode.

- **With a stylus** only the stylus draws; your fingers keep turning pages and
  scrolling, so your palm on the screen does no harm.
- **Without a stylus** switch on **Draw with finger** in the toolbar's menu (or in
  **Settings** → **Screen & live mode**). Fingers then draw instead of turning pages.
- The **foot pedal and the keyboard** keep turning pages while you draw.
- With a mouse (e.g. in the web client), the mouse draws.

## The tools

- **Pen** – a line; with a stylus it gets wider the harder you press.
- **Highlighter** – a translucent wide line behind the text.
- **Text** – tap a spot and type with the keyboard. **Enter** stores the text,
  **Shift+Enter** starts a new line; with the on-screen keyboard, store it with the
  check mark next to the field. Escape drops what you typed. Tap a text with the text
  tool to change it; emptying it deletes it. Colour and stroke width set the colour
  and size of the text.
- **Eraser (whole stroke)** – removes every stroke it touches. The back of a stylus
  erases too.
- **Eraser (part of a stroke)** – cuts away only what it touches.
- **Select (lasso)** – draw around strokes, stamps and texts to select them. Then drag them to another
  place, tap a colour to recolour them, or use **Smaller**, **Larger** and **Delete
  selection**. To pick a single mark, just tap it; touch it and drag to move it right away.
- **Stamps** – tap to place a sign: music marks (fermata, breath mark, crescendo,
  decrescendo, ritardando, accent), repeats (×2, ×3, ×4, repeat signs, coda, segno)
  and cues (watch out, stop, look, important, star).
- **Colour** and **Line width** apply to new strokes and to the selection.
- **Undo** and **Redo** go back and forth through your changes since you started
  drawing.

The colours stay readable on the dark and black stage appearance: each colour has a
lighter shade there.

## Same view, exact place

Annotations stay exactly where you drew them as long as the song looks the same –
on the same device, in the same orientation, with the same text size and the same
sequence. On another device, after turning the tablet or with a changed song, each
stroke follows the line of the song it was drawn next to and grows or shrinks with
the text. Free arrows then may no longer point exactly at their target; a hint in
the top corner says that the annotations were drawn for another view (e.g. in
landscape).

If a line was removed from the song, its annotations show faded while you draw, with
a hint; move them with the lasso or delete them.

Annotations on PDF sheet music stay on their page and never move.

Each song (and each PDF) holds about 1 MB of annotations, which is thousands of strokes. From
80 % on, drawing mode shows how full it is; when it is full, new strokes are not kept
until you erase some.

## Show, hide and delete

- The eye in the control bar shows or hides all your annotations.
- In the song's personal panel (the person icon) you see how many annotations the
  song has, can hide them for this song only, or **Delete all annotations of this
  song** – with **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **Settings** → **Display** → **Show freehand annotations** switches them off
  everywhere.

The song screen shows your annotations too, next to their lines; you draw in live
mode only.
