If you play keys, setDING can switch your keyboard to the right sound for every song
in live mode: piano for one song, organ for the next. You store your **keyboard
program** with each song; setDING sends it to the keyboard by MIDI as soon as the
song is up on stage.

The program is **yours only**: it is a [personal adjustment](/en/songs/personal/),
the same in all your bands and on all your devices. Nobody else in the band sees or
notices it.

## Where it works

- **Android, Windows and Linux** with a USB cable to the keyboard.
- **In the browser** in **Chrome** and **Edge**; the first time, the browser asks
  whether setDING may use MIDI devices.
- **Not yet on iPhone and iPad**, and not in Safari. Use an Android or Windows device
  for MIDI.

Bluetooth MIDI is not supported yet.

## Setting up the keyboard

1. Under **Settings** → **Musician**, choose **Keyboard** as **My instrument**.
   Only then is there a **Keyboard (MIDI)** category; with another instrument
   setDING sends nothing, but your settings and programs are kept.
2. Connect the keyboard to the tablet or computer by **USB** (on a tablet often with
   a USB OTG adapter).
3. Open **Settings** → **Keyboard (MIDI)** and switch on **MIDI program changes**.
   These settings apply to this device only.
4. Choose your keyboard under **Output**. If setDING does not find it, tap **Search
   again**. setDING remembers the keyboard and connects to it again by itself when
   you plug it in later.
5. Choose the **Device profile** from the list:
   - **Generic** – bank select and program number, for any keyboard. Its manual
     tells you which values it expects.
   - **Nord Stage 3** – you enter programs as the Nord's display shows them.
6. Set the **MIDI channel** the keyboard receives on.

### Nord Stage 3

- On the Nord, switch on **"Program Change"** reception in its **MIDI menu**.
- In setDING, set the **MIDI channel** to the Nord's **global channel**.
- For each song's program you choose what the Nord calls up:
  - **Program** – bank A–P, page 1–5 and button 1–5, as the display shows e.g.
    `K:33`.
  - **Live memory** – one of the five live memories.
  - **Song** – a Nord song and its part 1–5.
- The numbering of Nord songs depends on the Nord's OS version (checked with OS
  2.54). If a song does not match, use the profile **Generic**.

## Storing a song's program

1. Open the song and tap **Personal adjustments**.
2. Under **Keyboard program**, tap **Choose program** and pick it without typing,
   e.g. bank C and 13 in the grid (page 1, button 3), then **Save**. With the
   Nord, first choose **Program**, **Live memory** or **Song** at the top; a new
   program starts with the kind you saved last.
3. **Test** sends it to the keyboard right away, so you hear whether the sound is
   right.

The field appears when MIDI program changes are on for this device or when the song
already has a program. You can remove the program again.

## In live mode

- As soon as a song is **up**, setDING sends its program: when live mode starts and
  whenever another song becomes current – whether you turn, tap or jump in the set
  overview.
- The song has to stay on screen for about **a third of a second**. If you turn
  quickly past several songs, the keyboard does not switch for each of them.
- Turning pages **within a song** sends nothing.
- A song **without a program**, a **break** or a **text** in the set sends nothing;
  the keyboard keeps its sound.
- If the next song has the same program, it is sent again anyway. If you switched
  the sound by hand on the keyboard in the meantime, it is right again.
- A small **keyboard icon** at the bottom right shows whether the keyboard is
  connected; crossed out and red means **not connected**. The icon appears only
  while MIDI program changes are on.
- Tap the middle of the page, then the keyboard icon **Send program again**, e.g.
  after the keyboard was restarted.

Errors never interrupt the show: setDING shows no message and turning pages works as
usual. The **diagnostic log** under **Settings** → **Storage & info** says what went
wrong.
