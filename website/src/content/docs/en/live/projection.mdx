At small gigs, worship sessions or church services the audience wants to sing
along. setDING shows the lyrics for that **without chords** in a browser: on the
laptop at the projector, on a TV or as a source in OBS. You need no other software
and no second song collection. The projection follows the set you play in live
mode.

The tablet in live mode serves a small web page in the **local network** for it.
No internet is needed; the tablet and the device at the projector only have to be
in the same Wi-Fi.

## Where it works

- **Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows and Linux** – in the installed app.
- **Not in the web client** (`app.setding.app`): a browser cannot serve a page to
  other devices.
- The device at the projector only needs a **browser**; nothing to install there.

## Start the projection

1. Start **live mode** and tap the middle of the page to show the controls.
2. Tap **Project lyrics**.

<Image src={start} alt="Live mode with the controls open; on the left the Project lyrics button" />

3. setDING shows a **QR code** and the addresses. Open the address under **Full
   screen** in the browser of the device at the projector – with the QR code or by
   typing it. The copy button lets you send it on.

<Image src={sheet} alt="The Lyrics projection sheet with the QR code, the addresses for full screen and the lower third, and Stop projection" />

4. In the browser, the button at the bottom right (it shows when you move the
   mouse) or a **double click** switches to **full screen**.

The page shows the current section as large as it fits, white on black, the
**section** small above it (e.g. *Verse 1*, *Chorus*) and the song's copyright
below. A section without a name of its own shows its kind, as in live mode
(*Verse*, *Chorus*, *Bridge*).

<Image src={page} alt="The projected page: a verse in large white letters on black, the copyright small below" />

The address contains a random key and **stays the same** on this device: you set
up the projector browser and the OBS source only once. It works only while
projecting. To lock out old links, tap **New address** (in the sheet or under
[settings](#settings) → **New projection address**). The projection ends with
**Stop projection** or when you leave live mode.

## What is projected

- A song is split into **slides**, one per section (verse, chorus, bridge …). A
  long section is spread over several slides.
- The order is the **played** one: the set item's sequence, otherwise the band's
  or the song's (see [Key and sequence](/en/sets/key-and-sequence/)). A chorus
  played several times appears several times.
- **Without** chords, comments, tabs and grids – the lyrics only.
- A **marker** in the set (e.g. *Break*) shows a black slide or, if you set it,
  its text.

## Following and advancing by hand

While projecting, the **projection bar** sits at the bottom of the stage. In the
middle it shows which section is projected and where you are (e.g. *Chorus* above
*3/8*).

<Image src={bar} alt="Live mode with the projection bar at the bottom centre: addresses, copyright hint, previous slide, slide 2/6, next slide, follow, blank" />

- **Automatically:** a new song starts at its first slide. When you turn a page in
  a song with several pages, the projection jumps to the first section of the new
  page.
- **By hand:** when a song fits one page, move on with **‹** and **›** in the bar –
  or someone else takes the tablet. Once you advance by hand, the projection no
  longer follows live mode for this song. **Follow live mode again** (the round
  arrows) turns that back on; with the next song it follows anyway.
- **With the page turner:** turn on **Turning pages moves the slides** in the
  [settings](#settings). Then **forward** (page turner, keyboard, tapping on the
  right) first goes to the next slide of the song and only after the last one to
  the next song; **back** likewise, into the previous song at its last slide. When
  the song has several pages, live mode turns along so you see what is projected.

  <Image src={turns} alt="The setting Turning pages moves the slides, switched on" />
- **Blank the projection** (the screen) makes the projection black, e.g. for a
  prayer or an announcement, until you tap it again.
- The **cast symbol** shows the addresses again.

When you open the controls, the projection bar makes room for them; the
**Projection: addresses** button in the controls then shows the addresses.

## Copyright

Projecting lyrics in public requires the copyright. setDING shows the song's
`{copyright: …}` as written (see [ChordPro](/en/songs/chordpro/)). When it is
missing, an orange **©** appears in the projection bar: the song is then projected
without a copyright. Add it to the song.

## Two languages

For multilingual services the projection can show a **second language** beside the
lyrics: choose it in the [settings](#settings). When the song has a [language
version](/en/songs/library/#language-versions) in that language, it stands to the
right in landscape and below in portrait, a little lighter. The sections are paired
in order.

<Image src={twoLanguages} alt="The projected page with the German verse on the left and the English version on the right, a little lighter" />

## Lower third for livestreams (OBS)

The second address in the sheet, **Lower third (OBS)**, shows the lyrics in a band
at the bottom on a **transparent** background. Add it in OBS (or similar software)
as a **browser source** above the camera picture. Set **Lines per slide** to 2 to 4
for it.

<Image src={lowerThird} alt="The lower third: two lines of lyrics in a dark band at the bottom, the copyright on the right in the band; the grey background stands for the camera picture" />

## Settings

Under **Settings** → **Screen & live mode** → **Projection** you choose how the
projection looks. The settings apply to **this device** and take effect at once,
also while projecting.

<Image src={settings} alt="The Projection settings: colours, font, alignment, lines per slide, copyright and second language" />

- **Colours:** white on black or black on white.
- **Font** and **alignment** (centred or left).
- **Lines per slide:** at most this many lines on a slide.
- **Show the section:** the name of the section small above the lyrics (on).
- **Copyright:** on every slide or on the first and last one only.
- **Second language:** see [Two languages](#two-languages).
- **Turning pages moves the slides:** see
  [Following and advancing by hand](#following-and-advancing-by-hand).
- **New projection address:** locks out old links; the sheet shows the new one.
- **Markers as text:** shows the text of a marker instead of a black slide.
- **Show the next slide:** small below the lyrics, e.g. for a screen only the
  band sees.

## When it does not work

- **The page does not load:** many guest Wi-Fis do not let devices talk to each
  other. Turn on the tablet's **hotspot** and connect the projector device to it,
  or bring your own router.
- **No network found:** the tablet is in no Wi-Fi. Connect it or turn on its
  hotspot.
- **Windows** asks at the first start whether setDING may accept connections.
  Allow it for **private networks**.
- **iPhone and iPad** project only while setDING is in the foreground. Live mode
  keeps the screen on anyway.
- **The projector laptop goes to sleep:** the browser cannot keep it awake through
  this address. Turn off sleep and the screen saver there.
- **A red dot** at the top right of the projected page means the connection to the
  tablet is lost. The last slide stays and the page reconnects by itself.
  If you created a **New address**, open the new one.

The page shows only what is projected right now. Nobody can read your songs or
change anything through it, and it is reachable in the local network only.
