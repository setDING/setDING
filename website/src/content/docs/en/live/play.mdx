Live mode shows a song in full screen, as large as possible, and turns pages with a
tap, a swipe or a foot pedal. The screen stays on while live mode is open.

<Image src={live} alt="Live mode: a song in three columns with chords, a personal note and chord diagrams" />

## Start live mode

- **A set:** open the set and tap **Live** at the top right. You go through all the
  songs and markers of the set.
- **A single song:** open the song and tap **Live** at the top right.

The coloured **Live** button is always at the far right of the title bar, set apart
from the other buttons. On a phone it shows only the play icon.

Leave it with **Exit live mode** in the control bar or the Escape key.

## Turn pages

| Forward | Back |
|---|---|
| Tap the right third of the screen | Tap the left third |
| Swipe left | Swipe right |
| Right arrow, down arrow, Page Down, Space | Left arrow, up arrow, Page Up |

setDING fits every song to the screen: the sections are arranged in columns and the
text is as large as possible. A long song is split into several pages; "forward"
turns through the pages of the song first, then to the next item of the set. The
bottom bar shows where you are, e.g. **Page 1/2**, and which song comes next.

### With a foot pedal

Bluetooth page turners (foot pedals) connect to the device as a keyboard. Pair the
pedal in your device's Bluetooth settings; setDING understands the keys above without
further setup. One press turns exactly once, even if you hold the pedal down.

Under **Settings › Screen & live mode › Page turner** you can **pair** your
pedal: tap **Pair** and press the pedal. setDING remembers the device, and
[Ready to play](/en/start/overview/#ready-to-play) on the overview then shows
before the gig whether your pedal is connected. Use **⋮** to pair it again or
forget it.

<Image src={pageTurner} alt="The Page turner setting with the paired pedal AirTurn DUO, connected" />

This works on Android and Windows. An iPad only tells whether a keyboard is
connected, not which one; on the web and on Linux it is not possible. There,
Ready to play shows when the page turner was last used.

## The control bar

Tap the middle of the screen to show or hide the control bar:

- **Exit live mode**
- **Set overview** – all items of the set; tap one to jump straight to it, e.g. when
  you change the order on the spot.
- **Key** – in a set, the key of this set for the whole band (**Key in this set –
  for the whole band**); for a single song only your own (**My key – just for me**).
- **Smaller text** and **Larger text** – for you and this song.
- **Fit to page** – fits the song to the screen again.
- **C** / **1** / **I** – shows the chords as names, as Nashville numbers or as Roman
  numerals. Numbers and numerals need a key in the song (see
  [Chords, Nashville and numerals](/en/songs/chord-views/)).
- **Chords in verses** – all chords, chords in verse 1 only, or verses stacked line
  by line under their chords (see [Personal adjustments](/en/songs/personal/)). Only
  for songs where this changes something.

## What else you see

- **Song header:** the title, the key as you play it tonight, capo, tempo and time
  signature. If a song sequence applies, setDING shows only its parts and names the
  hidden ones.
- **Progress bar:** a thin bar above the bottom bar shows the songs of the set and
  the pages of the current song, easy to see from the corner of your eye. Markers
  are dashed.
- **Markers** such as breaks, announcements or the encore get a page of their own
  with their icon in large type.

<Image src={liveMarker} alt="Live mode: the page of a marker with a coffee cup icon and the text Break" />
- **PDF sheet music** is turned with the same gestures, page by page.

Changes that arrive from the server during the gig are applied only when you leave
live mode, so nothing changes unexpectedly on stage.

Every band member turns the pages on their own device; the devices are not linked.

## Tips for the gig

- Check beforehand that the set has no note such as **1 song not offline** and
  there is no sync warning above the sets (see [Offline ready](/en/sets/share/)).
- Plug the tablet into its charger. setDING keeps the screen on, but not the battery
  full. If **The screen may turn off – check the device's power settings** appears,
  set a longer screen timeout on the device.
- Android's screen pinning keeps you from leaving the app by accident.
- In **Settings** → [**Screen & live mode**](/en/settings/display/#screen--live-mode) you set the font size, columns, half-page
  turns and the appearance on stage, e.g. **Black** for OLED screens.
