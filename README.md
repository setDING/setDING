# RealChord

**Your band's songbook – on every tablet, on every stage, even without Wi-Fi.**

No more stacks of paper, outdated chord sheets or "which key are we playing this
in?". With RealChord the band leader puts the set together once, and every member
has it on their own tablet: the right songs, in the right order, in the right key.
On stage you play from a clean, large display and turn pages with your foot.

[**Download the latest release →**](https://github.com/tafli/RealChord/releases/latest)

## Why RealChord?

### 🎸 Made for the stage
- **Live mode** in full screen; the screen stays on for the whole gig.
- **Turn pages with a foot pedal** (Bluetooth), the keyboard, a tap or a swipe –
  your hands stay on your instrument.
- At the end of a song, "next" takes you straight to the next song of the set.
- **Works completely offline.** No network, no login, no loading on stage.

### 🎼 Sets the whole band shares
- Put a set together in minutes: drag & drop songs, add breaks and notes such as
  an announcement or the encore.
- Set the **key and the sequence per song** for tonight's gig.
- Every member gets the set on their own device, and RealChord shows when it is
  **ready to play offline**.
- Print the set list as a PDF or share it as text.

### 🎹 Every musician sees it their way
- **Transpose** with one tap, set a **capo**, change the font size.
- Chord names in **B or H** notation, or as **Nashville numbers**.
- Personal notes and adjustments are **yours alone** – they never change the song
  for the rest of the band.
- Chord diagrams for guitar, ukulele, keyboard and more; parts only for your
  instrument or your voice.

### 📄 Your songs, your format
- Songs are written in **[ChordPro](https://www.chordpro.org/)**, the open standard
  for chord sheets – no lock-in, import and export any time.
- Import `.cho`, `.chordpro`, `.chopro` and `.txt` files, one by one or as a ZIP –
  including the ChordPro files you download from SongSelect.
- Attach **PDF sheet music** to any song; it is on every device, offline too.
- A built-in editor with syntax highlighting, autocompletion and a live preview.

### 👥 For bands, schools and churches
- Several **bands in one organization** share one song library – ideal for music
  schools, churches and anyone with more than one band.
- **Invite members** with a link or a QR code.
- Admins manage songs and sets; members play (and edit, if you allow it).
- Manage songs and sets from any computer in the **web app**, no installation needed.

### 🔒 Your data stays yours
- RealChord syncs with your **own, self-hosted server** – your songs are not stored
  with a third party.
- Open source under the MIT licence.

## Get RealChord

| Platform | How |
|---|---|
| **Android** | Install [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), tap *Add app* and enter `https://github.com/tafli/RealChord`. Obtainium installs RealChord and keeps it up to date. |
| **Windows** | Download `realchord-<version>-windows-x64.zip` from the [releases](https://github.com/tafli/RealChord/releases/latest), unpack it and start `realchord.exe`. |
| **Linux** | Download `realchord-<version>-linux-x64.tar.gz` from the [releases](https://github.com/tafli/RealChord/releases/latest), unpack it and start `realchord`. |
| **iOS** | Coming later. |

The app speaks **English and German** (Swiss Standard German).

**Want to see what it can do?** Import
[`feature-showcase.zip`](docs/examples/feature-showcase.zip) in the app
(*Songs → Import files*) – one song that shows off every ChordPro feature.
